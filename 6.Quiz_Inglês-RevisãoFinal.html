Guia Rápido do Passado (Simple Past)

Verbos Regulares (+ed / +d)

celebrate → celebrated · visit → visited · open → opened · dance → danced · stay → stayed (com vogal antes do y!)

Verbos Irregulares

go → went · eat → ate · see → saw · win → won · give → gave · sing → sang · drink → drank · buy → bought · lose → lost

Negativa e Perguntas

Com didn’t ou Did...?, o verbo volta ao normal: didn’t go, Did she wear...?, Where did you go?

Lazer com go + -ing

No passado: went swimming, went hiking, went ice skating, went horseback riding, went fishing.
